Key findings

  • Leave use was widespread: 98.9% of employees submitted at least one application, so differences between groups are mainly about the amount and type of leave taken.
  • Entity MBAB carried the largest volume: 27,069 days, or 40.8% of all leave days; it also has the largest workforce.
  • IT had the highest department intensity: 14.8 leave days per employee, 114% above the overall average; Human Resource was lowest at 11.4 days per employee.
  • Annual leave dominated: it represented 71.2% of days, while sick leave accounted for 13.5% and maternity leave 15.4%.
  • Demand peaked in Jan: 6,908 leave days, 10.4% of the year; the lowest month was Dec at 3,876 days.

Recommendations

1. Plan for the annual peak.
Use the {peak_month[0]} demand pattern to set coverage plans and encourage earlier scheduling in high-volume teams.
2. Review IT leave intensity.
Check workload coverage and absence clustering in IT, where leave days per employee were highest.
3. Track leave mix by department.
Monitor annual, sick, and maternity leave separately so staffing plans reflect the type of absence, not only total days.
